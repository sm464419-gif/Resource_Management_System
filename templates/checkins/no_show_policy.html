No-show policy

Unused bookings keep other students and staff from using shared resources. Here's how we handle them.

If you can't make your booking, cancel it before it starts. Cancelled bookings never count as no-shows.

What counts as a no-show

A booking is a no-show when you haven't checked in within 15 minutes of the start time. The slot is then released to the next person.

  1. 15 min beforeCheck-in opens
  2. Start timeCheck in now to be on time
  3. +15 minCheck-in counts as late
  4. After +15 minMarked as a no-show

What happens after a no-show

Consequences build up over a rolling 30 days.

No-shows in 30 daysResult
1stWarning email. Nothing else changes.
2ndYou can only hold one active booking for 7 days.
3rd or moreBooking is suspended for 14 days. Staff review your account.

Excused absences

  • Resource was closed, broken, or locked when you arrived.
  • Class or exam schedule changed by the university.
  • Illness or emergency, with a note sent within 48 hours.

Common questions

I checked in late. Does that count as a no-show?

No. Checking in within 15 minutes after the start time is logged as late. Late check-ins don't lead to restrictions, but they appear in your check-in history.

How do I dispute a no-show?

Email the resource manager within 48 hours with your booking ID and a short reason. If the dispute is accepted, the no-show is removed from your record.

Does a group booking count against everyone?

No. The no-show is recorded against the person who made the booking.

When do old no-shows expire?

Each no-show stops counting 30 days after the booking date.
